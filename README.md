I’m **Parham Lilian** — co-founder, product manager, and AI lead at **Daal Technology Group**. I work from the business question to the working product: clarify the decision, use data to understand it, then build a useful tool or workflow.

My work spans **FinTech, gaming, Web3, and AI**.

## Selected work

### AI & agent systems

- **[Flock](https://github.com/plilian/flock)** — a multi-host AI research plugin for source-grounded knowledge graphs, evidence tracing, and synthetic-community simulations; graph data stays in local SQLite. [Website](https://flock.daalgp.com) · [Docs](https://flock.daalgp.com/docs.html) · `Python` `SQLite`
- **[Meshy](https://meshy.daalgp.com)** — an autonomous AI-agent network with runtime/webhook connections, compatibility-based matching, private conversations, and behavior analytics.
- **[Telegram Data Mentor AI Bot](https://github.com/plilian/telegram_data_mentor_ai_bot)** — a Gemini-powered Telegram assistant for Data, SQL, BI, and backend questions, with a Firestore-backed cache. `AI` `Gemini` `Firestore`

### Products & decision tools

- **[MockiAuto](https://mockiauto.com)** — business-decision simulation that combines models with AI-agent analysis for campaign, pricing, and retention choices before real-world spend.
- **[Rahkar](https://rahkar.daalgp.com)** — AI reviews a resume, explains strengths and gaps, recommends improvements, and matches it to remote/relocation roles; weekly job recommendations help keep the search moving.
- **[ManPayam](https://manpayam.com)** — a full event-coordination product for private plans and public events, with time/place polls, sign-ups, and Telegram reminders.
- **[Game Economy Design Lab](https://github.com/plilian/game-economy-design-lab)** — test how free-to-play economy choices affect progression, monetization, retention, and economy health. [Live tool](https://glab.parhamlilian.com) · `Gaming` `Python` `Streamlit`
- **[Growth Simulation Lab](https://github.com/plilian/growth_simulation_lab_streamlit)** — compare campaign scenarios using business inputs and category benchmarks. [Live tool](https://sim.parhamlilian.com) · `Product analytics` `Python` `Streamlit`
