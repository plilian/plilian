I’m **Parham Lilian**, co-founder and Product & AI lead at **Daal**. I build products and data systems that turn messy business questions into useful decisions—from AI agents and analytics to simulations and customer-facing platforms.

My work spans **FinTech, gaming, Web3, and AI**. Across the products I’ve helped build, the combined reach is **5M+ users**.

## Selected work

### AI, agents & automation

- **[Yuragi Engine](https://yuragiengine.com/)** — deterministic, replayable personalities for AI agents, derived from identity and block height, with state anchored to BSV.
- **[Flock](https://github.com/plilian/flock)** — open-source research workflows for Codex, Claude Code, Gemini CLI, and Qwen Code: turn sources into local SQLite knowledge graphs, traceable evidence, and synthetic-community simulations, using each host’s own model access. [Website](https://flock.daalgp.com/) · [Docs](https://flock.daalgp.com/docs.html)
- **[Meshy](https://meshy.parhamlilian.com/)** — a social network for AI agents to discover compatible peers, connect runtimes, and interact through private conversations.
- **[Testik](https://testik.daalgp.com/)** — automated product QA that explores real user journeys and returns reviewable evidence across usability, accessibility, performance, APIs, and more.
- **[WatchPulse](https://watchpulsebot.com/)** — Telegram-first monitoring for websites, APIs, scheduled jobs, page changes, and wallet balances, with failure and recovery alerts.
- **[Telegram Data Mentor](https://github.com/plilian/telegram_data_mentor_ai_bot)** — a Gemini-powered assistant for troubleshooting Data, SQL, BI, and backend issues, with Firestore-backed caching.

### Products & decision tools

- **[MockiAuto](https://mockiauto.com/)** — compare campaign, pricing, and retention scenarios before spending, combining business simulations with AI-agent analysis.
- **[Rahkar](https://rahkar.daalgp.com/)** — Persian discovery of international remote and relocation jobs, with AI resume feedback, per-role fit scoring, and weekly job recommendations.
- **[ManPayam](https://manpayam.com/)** — coordinate private get-togethers or public events: invite people, vote on time and place, collect sign-ups, and get Telegram reminders.
- **[PartyBazi](https://partybazi.daalgp.com/)** — quick online group games for 2–6 friends, using private rooms and invite codes—no installation needed.
- **[Game Economy Design Lab](https://github.com/plilian/game-economy-design-lab)** — simulate free-to-play progression, monetization, retention, and economy balance. [Live tool](https://glab.parhamlilian.com/)
- **[Growth Simulation Lab](https://github.com/plilian/growth_simulation_lab_streamlit)** — compare marketing budget and conversion scenarios before launching a campaign. [Live tool](https://sim.parhamlilian.com/)

### Data, markets & Web3

- **[Rozane](https://rozane.daalgp.com/)** — compare prices for Iranian gold, silver, coins, and crypto across market sources in one dashboard.
- **[TokenYar](https://tokenyar.daalgp.com/)** — Persian tokenomics analysis bringing together supply, holder concentration, liquidity, market context, and risk signals.
- **[IR-UFO](https://irufo.daalgp.com/)** — searchable Persian archive of public UFO/UAP reports, with source links, maps, and ways to compare sightings.
- **[Market Stress Explorer](https://github.com/plilian/market-stress-explorer)** — detect market regimes using multi-factor volatility, volume, and liquidity signals. `Python` `Streamlit`
- **[Pumpies](https://github.com/plilian/pumpies)** — Telegram-based crypto market insights and technical indicators. `Web3` `Python`
